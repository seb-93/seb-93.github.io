excerpt: 'Calving might be difficult leading to complications for cow and calf. Early detection of fetal distress could enable timely interventions such as caesarean section and improve animal welfare. While fetal monitoring is standard in human medicine, its application in veterinary practice remains limited. This project aims to develop a low-cost prenatal monitoring system for detecting fetal stress in cattle before and during parturition. Using fetal ECG (fECG) signals, the study evaluates wearable technology combined with predictive modeling to create a non- invasive, user-friendly system  to monitor fetal heart activity.'
